Four formats, each on its own stack. Rebuilt from its own prompt.

Each type was studied from one of your published originals: its tech stack, layout, design tokens, components and interaction model. That study became the type's prompt, and the rebuild shown here was built from that prompt.

Rebuild and original, side by side. Thumbnails marked "orig" are screenshots of your original; the rest are the rebuild. Open the rebuild to use it. Only the FutureSE talk uses the cisco-3d-stage skill, because that talk is where the skill came from. The other three have no deck navigation, no presenter camera box and no narrated talk track.
